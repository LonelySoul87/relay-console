# Relay Console v2.5.0 language audit

Date: 2 September 2026

Status: reciprocal agent review complete. Native-speaker feedback remains
welcome after publication but is not a release blocker.

## Scope

This pass read every German and Arabic catalog entry and exercised the runtime
surfaces where grammar depends on surrounding text: completion summaries,
recovery notices, score labels, transcript exports, review packets, prompts,
accessibility names, and code-format instructions.

Catalog parity and placeholder checks remain separate from this review. They
prove structural completeness, but they do not prove natural wording.

## Arabic corrections

- Kept the shared two-answer and two-ballot labels in the nominative form, then
  rewrote the surrounding messages so those labels appear in grammatically
  standalone positions. This avoids inserting a nominative dual after a verb,
  preposition, or verbal noun that requires the form ending in `ين`.
- Replaced the three past-tense recovery reasons after `قبل أن` with present
  verbs: `تبدأ`, `تتجاهل`, and `تفتح`.
- Corrected the two-point label from `2 نقطة` to the dual `نقطتان`.
- Replaced the misleading use of `رمز` for programming code with the standard
  technical wording `تعليمات برمجية` and `شفرة`.
- Changed the counted-ballot export sentence from a governed construction to the
  standalone label `التصويتات المحتسبة: {ballots}`.

The other Arabic count forms were checked at the six Unicode CLDR categories.
The zero, one, few, many, and other forms remain appropriate. The participant
and round duals already appear only in standalone summaries and remain correct.

## German corrections

- Replaced recovery action names that incorrectly described the checkpoint as
  already restored.
- Rewrote the recovery notice and the privacy explanation in ordinary German.
- Restored the required article in `auf die gerenderte Darstellung`.
- Clarified the plan explanation, participant-role hint, keyboard note,
  tiebreaker role, and ranked-transcript label.
- Corrected prompt wording around quoted marker lines, synthesis, and revision.

The catalog remains consistently informal. No formal `Sie` address was
introduced.

## Evidence used

- Unicode CLDR Arabic plural categories and minimal pairs:
  https://unicode.org/cldr/charts/49/supplemental/language_plural_rules.html
- King Salman Global Academy for Arabic Language, including the rule that the
  dual is nominative with `ان` and accusative or genitive with `ين`:
  https://library.ksaa.gov.sa/links/epubs/tiqniaat_allughat_alearabiat_alhasubia.pdf
- Microsoft Learn Arabic terminology for code blocks:
  https://learn.microsoft.com/ar-sa/answers/support/markdown-format

## Acceptance boundary

This is an evidence-backed agent audit, not a claim that a fluent Arabic or
German human has approved every phrase. Native-speaker feedback remains useful
for tone and regional preference and can be reported through the public issue
forms without including private relay content. Screen-reader listening quality
and physical touch behavior are also tracked through post-release feedback
because they require capabilities beyond catalog inspection and the available
development hardware.
