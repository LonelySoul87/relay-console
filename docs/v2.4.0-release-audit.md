## Browser and accessibility evidence

The final artifact was served from a clean local HTTP origin after migration.
At 390 by 844 CSS pixels, the setup page fit in English, French, and Spanish
with no horizontal overflow. A clean Draft and improve relay started, saved and
advanced through Ctrl+Enter, preserved the captured answer, and reported the
correct transcript filter count.

At 320 by 700 CSS pixels, the relay still had no horizontal overflow. The
shortcut dialog fit without internal scrolling, moved focus to its heading,
and returned focus to the trigger on Escape. The updated landing page also fit
at that width and exposed all six feature cards. The browser console remained
free of warnings and errors throughout.

Earlier reciprocal passes additionally covered import review; simultaneous
resume and recovery banners; preset management; transcript filters; mouse and
keyboard lane navigation; focus return; and high reflow pressure.

Real mouse and keyboard input confirmed the input-specific lane focus behavior.
A click with no pointer detail, the route used by assistive technology, is also
covered directly by the regression suite.

Not claimed as completed:

- A genuine touch tap on a physical touch device.
- A listening session with NVDA or VoiceOver to judge announcement sequence by
  ear.

These are manual acceptance follow-ups, not concealed automated passes. The
known touch risk is limited to where focus lands after navigation, not whether
the turn changes or whether the answer remains reachable.
